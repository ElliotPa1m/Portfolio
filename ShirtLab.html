This is ShirtLab, an e-commerce shop that sells t-shirts with diffrent prints on the front, back or both.
This is the first group project in the backend development course, we were a three person group for this project.
You can choose what membership level you want, the higher the level the more you get from the website, more t-shirts and more choices for prints.
